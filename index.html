Speed Test Marking Formula

  • Hindi passage = 400 words; each correct word = 0.0625 marks → Marks = correct words × 0.0625 (max 25)
  • English passage = 500 words; each correct word = 0.05 marks → Marks = correct words × 0.05 (max 25)
  • 144 correct Hindi words ≈ 14.4 WPM = 9 marks (qualify); 400 words = 40 WPM = full 25
  • 180 correct English words = 18 WPM = 9 marks; 500 words = 50 WPM = full 25
  • No negative marking. A "word" = characters between two spaces.

A word earns 0 marks if it has:

  • Wrong character, word or digit
  • Omitted character, word or digit
  • Repeated character, word, digit or line
  • Punctuation not reproduced exactly
  • Word-spacing error
  • Wrong capital/small letter (English)
  • Wrong Hindi matra - including invisible duplicate matras the software detects (e.g. केवल = dsoy is correct, dsssssoy is not; प = i, not Ik)

Exam Hall Rules (Speed Papers)

  • Backspace works only inside the word being typed - once you press Space, the word is final. This app enforces the same rule.
  • Font and keyboard layout are pre-selected; Alt/Ctrl shortcuts are not allowed in the exam software. (This app still lets you enter Alt-code glyphs like क्र / क्त because Remington needs them.)
  • SC/ST candidates get up to 5% relaxation in the minimum marks of each paper.
  • Typing marks are added to final merit - every mark above 9 improves rank.
